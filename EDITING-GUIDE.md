# Editing the Transform Fremantle website

You can change almost everything on the website yourself. You don't need to know
anything technical, and you can't break it in a way that can't be undone.

---

## Signing in

Go to **transformfreo.com/admin** and click **Sign In with GitHub**.

A window pops up asking you to approve. Say yes. You'll land in the editor.

You need a GitHub account with access to the site. If you don't have one yet,
whoever set the site up can add you.

Bookmark that address. It's the only one you need.

---

## What you'll see

Down the left is a list of what you can change:

- **Pages** — the words and photos on each of the five pages
- **Resource booklets** — the PDFs people download
- **Site-wide** — the logo, the menu, and the footer that appear on every page

Click any of them, change what you want, and press **Save**.

---

## What happens when you save

Your change goes live in about a minute or two. Save, wait a moment, then
refresh the website to see it.

If it hasn't appeared after a few minutes, something's wrong — see the last
section.

---

## Common jobs

### Change a prayer meeting time

**Pages → About Us → Prayer meetings**

Click the meeting you want to change. Edit the time. Save.

### Add or remove a meeting

Same place. Use **Add meeting** at the bottom of the list, or the bin icon on a
meeting that has stopped.

You can drag meetings to reorder them. They appear on the website in the order
they're listed here.

If a meeting is planned but not running yet, tick **Mark as "Coming Soon"**
instead of deleting it.

### Change the phone number

**Pages → Connect → Phone number**

There are two boxes. The first is how the number looks on the page — spaces are
fine. The second is the same number with no spaces, which is what makes it
tappable on a phone. Change both.

You only enter the number here. It updates everywhere it appears.

### Change the bank details

**Pages → Connect → Giving & bank details**

The BSB must be six digits and the account number five to ten. The editor will
tell you if something doesn't look right.

Read these back twice before saving. It's the one thing on the site where a typo
costs real money.

### Add a booklet

**Resource booklets**

Click **Add booklet**, choose your PDF, and give it a title. The title is what
people see on the website, so write it properly — "What is Salvation? And What
Should I do Next?" rather than a filename.

Drag booklets to change the order they appear in.

To take a booklet down without deleting it, tick **Hide from the website**. You
can put it back later.

### Change a photo

Any **Background photo** box lets you upload a new image or pick one you've used
before.

Wide, landscape photos work best. The heading sits over the middle of the image,
so avoid pictures with something important in the centre.

### Change the words on a page

Every heading and paragraph on the site has a box in here. Find the page under
**Pages**, find the box, type.

Where you see a list of paragraphs, each entry is its own paragraph on the page.
Use **Add paragraph** for a new one rather than putting blank lines inside an
existing box.

---

## Things worth knowing

**You can't lose anything.** Every change is recorded with the date and who made
it. Anything can be put back the way it was.

**The menu names can change, the pages can't.** Under Site-wide you can rename a
menu item. Adding or removing a whole page needs a developer.

**Empty boxes leave gaps.** If you clear a heading, the page shows a blank space
where it used to be. Put something in it or ask for that section to be removed
properly.

**Photos should be a sensible size.** Very large files make the site slow to
load on a phone. Anything up to about 2000 pixels wide is plenty.

---

## If something goes wrong

**You can't sign in.** Check you're using the right GitHub account. If it still
won't let you in, your access may not be set up yet.

**You saved but the site hasn't changed.** Give it two minutes and refresh. If
it's still not there, a save may have failed — try it again.

**You've made a mess and want to start over.** Don't try to fix it by guessing.
Every past version is saved, so it can be restored exactly. Ask for help.

**Something looks broken on the page.** Take a screenshot and note which page.
That's usually enough for someone to sort it quickly.
